How to remove the background from an anime image

  1. Drop, paste or choose a PNG, JPG or WebP image. The AI cuts out the character.
  2. Keep it Transparent, or pick White, a Color or your own background Image.

FAQ

Is this background remover really free?

Yes. There is no account, no limit on the number of images and no watermark.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The AI model is downloaded once (about 88 MB, then cached by your browser) and runs on your own GPU through WebGPU, or on the CPU if WebGPU is not available. Images never leave your device.

What kind of images work best?

Anime, manga, illustrations, game art and other drawn characters. It uses an anime segmentation model (ISNet trained by SkyTNT), so it handles hair, outlines and stylized shading better than photo-trained removers. Real photos are not its strength.

Does it keep the original resolution?

Yes. The download has the same size as your image, up to about 40 megapixels (for example 8000×5000). The preview on this page is scaled down.

Which browsers are supported?

Recent Chrome and Edge on Windows, macOS, Linux and Android use the GPU and take a few seconds per image. Browsers without WebGPU fall back to CPU mode, which takes around 20 seconds or more.

Can I make the image bigger too?

Yes. Use the free AI image upscaler to enlarge anime art 2x or 4x, also in your browser.
